# Brag Plan: Quorum

## What is this app?
Quorum is a focus group in a box: describe any business concept, pick any street in the world, and an agent convenes a panel of that neighborhood — every panelist built from measured taste data (Qloo's 250M-entity graph), every reaction citing its receipt — and hands you a GO / PIVOT / NO-GO verdict before you sign the lease.

## The angle
The most expensive guess in hospitality is *right concept, wrong street* — a lease is three years, a real focus group costs $10–40k. Quorum runs one in minutes, and unlike every AI "simulated user" toy, its panelists can't make things up: the receipts are right there under each sentence. The video plays it straight and premium — the restraint IS the flex.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant serif line on bone paper: **"The lease is three years."** beat → **"The panel, three minutes."** — the app's own hero copy, typed with the em-dash landing like a gavel. It earns the next 17 seconds because it states the stake and the promise in one breath.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The concept, typed.** The PromptBar-style input fills character by character: "a natural wine bar with vinyl listening nights" — starter chips fade under it. Cursor ticks.
2. **The street.** One field, one keystroke: "Rainey Street, Austin" — then the convening pulse (the app's breathing dot) with the agent's live line: "scanning all 10 Qloo domains".
3. **The panel with receipts.** Four transcript turns slide in one by one — enthusiast ("You had me at Austin Street Brewery…"), skeptic ("Convince me this is worth changing my routine for."), each with its receipt line underneath: entity · domain · affinity 0.91. The receipts stamp in like evidence tags.
4. **The verdict dashboard.** Giant serif **GO** in muted green lands with the meters filling: concept–street fit, panel grounding rate 100% receipt-cited.

## Outro / punchline
The lift line, quietly: **"Street lift +2.2 pts — this street amplifies your concept."** → cut to wordmark: *Quorum.* with the tag "Grounded hypotheses. Real people to call." and quorum-akshay.vercel.app.

## User flow worth showing
entry (type concept) → key action (pick street, convene panel) → result (verdict dashboard with receipt-cited transcript). The centerpiece is scenes 1–4: the working app, not marketing.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "quiet premium product film — editorial serif on bone paper; the product's honesty is the punchline"
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds, soft crossfades. Type does the acting. No flash, no zoom-slop; every claim lands as calmly as a receipt.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm minimal bed with sparse, precise accents — a premium film, not a hype edit
- Music: soft minimal piano/electronic bed, low in the mix
- Music treatment: fade in over 1s, sit low throughout, swell gently at the verdict, fade out under the wordmark
- Music cue guidance: cues detected at composition time; target strong cues for the hook's second line, the first receipt stamp, and the verdict landing
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — this tone stays still and confident
- SFX posture: sparse; soft key ticks while the concept types, one quiet "stamp"/tick per receipt line, a single low thud under the verdict serif
- Audio-coupled moments: typed concept text, receipt lines stamping in sequence, verdict meter fills
- Restraint rule: no risers, no whooshes, nothing louder than the bed; silence between receipt stamps is allowed
